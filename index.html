Porch Talk Uploader

Publishing tool for the Porch Talk Pro and Plática de Porche channels

Porch Talk Uploader is a private tool run by the owner of two YouTube channels. Each day it prepares a short how-to video in English and Spanish and uploads it to the owner's own channels through the YouTube Data API.

It isn't offered to the public: the only Google account it connects to is the channel owner's, and it only uploads videos. It doesn't read, collect or store anyone else's data.
